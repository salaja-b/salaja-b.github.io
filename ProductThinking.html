Product Thinking

Product thinking made fun, practical, & profitable.

What is product thinking?

Product thinking is the habit of starting from the real problem a person has, and working backward to a solution — instead of falling in love with a feature and looking for somewhere to use it.

Why it matters

Great products aren't just well-built; they're well-aimed. Product thinking is how you make sure the thing you build is the thing worth building.

How I approach it

Start with the user. Get specific about the problem. Ship something small, learn from it, and improve. Keep it fun along the way.
